How it works

Your morning, handled — starting the night before.

Set your shift once. From there the app runs the same drill a careful person would — except it never forgets, never oversleeps, and never does the math wrong at midnight.

🌙

Night before it re-arms itself

Tomorrow's alarm is computed from your shift start, your getting-ready time, and your real drive — not a guess you typed at 1 AM. Survives reboots and phone updates.

📍

It checks where you actually are PRO

Slept at a friend's? Third shift, crashing at odd hours? Sleepover mode notices you're not home and rebuilds the wake math from where you really are. Nothing to toggle — it just knows.

🛣️

It watches the road while you sleep PRO

Live traffic before you wake. A wreck on the interstate means an earlier alarm with the reason attached — not a write-up in your file.

🔔

It gets you up. Period.

Alarm-stream audio that ignores your mute switch, with an escalating Can't-Miss mode that gets louder and rotates tones until you deal with it. Free for everyone — waking up isn't a luxury.

💬

Rough morning? The message writes itself.

Running behind, it drafts a professional heads-up with your real ETA — matched to whoever's reading it: boss, team lead, or HR. You always review and press send yourself.
