Delete your account & data

Effective date: August 3, 2026

My Bad Boss AI is published by LuxWaveLabs LLC. This page explains how to permanently delete your account and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Bad Boss AI.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Under About, tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@mybadboss.app from the email address linked to your account (if you secured it), or include any account details you have. We verify and delete within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account identity (authentication user).
  • Server-side data associated with the account: your profile and Pro entitlement state, service-usage records (daily AI and maps allowances), any saved work-routine times (label, start time, days — coordinates are never uploaded), and draft logs from the call-intercept flow.
  • On-device data — your routines, contacts, message history, settings, and wake-screen photo — is wiped from your phone by the in-app deletion. (Contacts and routines live only on your device; they are never stored on our servers.)

What this does not cover

  • Your Pro subscription is billed by Google Play, not by us. Cancel it separately in the Play Store → Subscriptions to stop future charges. Deleting your account does not issue a refund.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying diagnostics (e.g. crash counts) that cannot be tied back to you may be retained.

Retention

Deletion is immediate for in-app requests and within 30 days for email requests. Backups are purged on their normal rotation. Accounts that never added an email and have been inactive for about 90 days are deleted automatically; accounts with an active paid subscription are never auto-deleted. Questions? Email support@mybadboss.app.
